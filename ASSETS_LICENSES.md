# CC0 asset trial: licences (branch `asset-trial`, experiment only, not on main)

Every model below is **CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)**. Commercial use, including a paid Steam game, is allowed with no attribution and no royalties. I checked the licence on each asset's own page on 2026-10-17 (UTC+7). No CC-BY assets are used, so no credit line is required. (Many Poly Pizza airliners are CC-BY 3.0, "Poly by Google" and others, and were skipped for that reason.)
All files are glTF 2.0 binary (.glb) and engine-agnostic, so they load as-is in three.js, Godot, Unity (glTFast) or Unreal (glTF importer).
