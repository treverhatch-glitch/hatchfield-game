# Hatchfield: more free assets (search 2026-10-17, UTC+7)

Branch `asset-trial`, experiment only. Files are in `public/asset_trial/more/<category>/` (about 930 MB, **untracked and not git-ignored**: don't `git add` the folder wholesale). Nothing is wired into the game yet.

Rules applied: only CC0, or CC-BY with a credit line (marked **CC-BY** below). No NC, ND, SA, Editorial, GPL or real airline livery. I checked every licence on the asset's own page (or the source's per-model JSON/API) on 2026-10-17. Triangles and sizes come from `@gltf-transform/core` in Node (instanced meshes counted per instance). Sizes are file sizes in MB.

Style target: the Innerscene narrow-body airliner (realistic, unbranded, real-world metres). **Innerscene** is the same family, so it's preferred wherever it has the item.

Recommendation key: **use** = drop-in candidate; **maybe** = usable with work or as a stand-in or LOD; **skip** = poor fit.

Licence and provenance notes
- Innerscene parts are CC0 ("Public domain (CC0)..."). People marked "adapted from MakeHuman CC0 assets" are still CC0. Innerscene lists an "available until" date (2030-2031) on each page, so keep our own copy.
- Grab3D is CC0. The JSON API flags each model as `procedural` or `ai-assisted`. AI-assisted ones need Steam's AI-content disclosure.
- amvlab (GitHub) is **CC BY 4.0**. Credit line: "Aircraft models by amvlab (github.com/amvlab/aircraft-models), CC BY 4.0, modified."
- Poly Pizza CC-BY 3.0 items need a credit line naming the creator listed in the table.
- NASA models are US Government work and "generally not subject to copyright in the United States", but they carry NASA/NOAA insignia and liveries, which are protected. They must be retextured before shipping, and the copyright waiver is US-only, so get legal sign-off. Treat them as maybe.
- Real type names (A380, 787, etc.) appear only in source filenames. In-game names should still be renamed before Steam, per the plan.

## Notable gaps
- **Realistic wide-bodies and jumbos:** no CC0 model exists that matches the Innerscene look (777, 787, A350, A330, A380, 747F, 767F, 757F). The quickest path is either the CC-BY Sketchfab models (needs a Sketchfab account; check for liveries) or building our own wide-body from the Innerscene narrow-body as a style reference.
- **Ground support equipment:** there's no realistic CC0 pushback tug, baggage tractor, carts or dollies, ULDs, belt loader, fuel truck, catering truck, stairs truck, GPU, de-icer, follow-me car or ARFF crash tender. The only complete set is the 3dassets.dev CC0 pack (stylised, AI-made, site down today). Stand-ins: Innerscene crew-cab pickup (follow-me), box truck (catering base) and fire pumper (ARFF).
- **Airport structures:** no realistic CC0 control tower, hangar, jet bridge, radar, windsock, runway or approach lights, or terminal modules. Innerscene's 12 m floodlight mast and fences exist only as STEP files.
- **Interior:** no security X-ray or body scanner, baggage carousel, airport self-service trolley or check-in desk with a bag belt. Queue stanchions, kiosks, e-gates, seating, shops and cafes are well covered.
- **People:** realistic MakeHuman people are 15-23 MB and 50-100k tris each, with one clip apiece, so they're unusable for crowds without heavy decimation. Quaternius (CC0, 24 clips, about 6k tris) stays the crowd solution, and there are now 8 more variants.
- **Textures:** asphalt, concrete and grass are well covered. There's no PBR set for airport-specific markings (yellow taxi centrelines, stand numbers, hold-short bars). Those should be decals we draw ourselves on top of Road007 or Poly Haven asphalt.
