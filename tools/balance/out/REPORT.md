## Method notes

- Daily capacity = servers × 3600 / serviceSec × ops hours / peak factor × (0.5 + 0.5 × condition).
- Served = min(demand, check-in, bags, security, boarding); bagged-pax share comes from unlocked airframes (bagVolume / seats).
- Retail capture drops when utilisation > 70% (queues eat dwell time). Happiness = 45 + comfort (diminishing 0.6^k per copy) − spill and queue penalties, and drives demand growth.
- Wear scales with utilisation; an item below 60% condition is repaired for 25% of its build cost.
- Field Level comes from FIELD_LEVEL_XP in catalog.ts; XP = served pax / 1000 × 3.
