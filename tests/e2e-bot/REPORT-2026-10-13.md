# Hatchfield QA bot report: Tue 13 Oct 2026, about 8:20 PM ICT

Bot: `tests/e2e-bot/bot.mjs` on branch `grok/qa` (see docs/QA.md). The bot ran headless Chromium with SwiftShader at 1280×800 against the Vite dev server.
Each run does the following: **New game → Passenger start → Starter kit (prefab I)** through the UI. Then it adds a heavy runway, a **second prefab terminal**, a Stand L, a hangar, cargo, police, a fire station and a helipad. It unlocks research (including heavy_pack and mil_ramp), signs the Guard agreement and signs 4 passenger and 2 cargo carriers. It then runs at **5× for 3 to 6 in-game days** (one day = 180 s of game clock) with a weighted fleet mix and forces **one A380 arrival per day**.

Branch coverage:
- **trunk** (`cursor/audit-playtest-562d`): the mixed and heavy scenarios.
- **#4 HUD**, **#5 logistics** and **#6 aircraft models** change the running game. For each one, the PR's runtime files were laid over the current trunk, which is a preview of the PR merged into trunk.
- **#2 audio, #3 branding, #7 features, #8 flow, #9 airlines and #10 cargo** only add isolated `src/<module>/` folders that `App`/`sim` doesn't import. Their playable build is the same as trunk's, so the bot can't test them. They need their own vitest suites. **Not run (N/A).**

### trunk (cursor/audit-playtest-562d)
1. **MEDIUM: prefab terminals degrade into missed flights under the bot's load.** missedFlights reached 120 by day 7 (mixed) and 85 by day 5 (heavy), with `hallOverloaded=true` and "Security bottleneck: 156 waiting". **lostPaxCount stayed 0 in every run**, which matches the design: an overloaded prefab degrades and doesn't lose anyone. The advisor upgrade prompt appeared ("Upgrade to Prefab II · Pier · $22,000").
2. **MEDIUM: the outbound bag ledger is short at takeoff.** In 17 of 41 mixed departures and 7 of 27 heavy departures, a passenger flight left with `bagsDeposited < bagUnits` and the bag stage still `inflight` or `pax_deposit`. Examples: A380 0/193, 18/161, 0/400; B738 20.8/97; A321 1.7/97 (stage `pax_deposit`). This lines up with missed check-ins in an overloaded hall, but the jet still leaves "loaded". Either bags should hold the departure, or they should become offloaded or lost bags and the ledger should say so. The news also shows "claim jammed: 161–195 bag units vs thin hall" on widebodies.
   *Repro:* run the heavy scenario and compare `f.bagsDeposited` to `f.bagUnits` when the phase becomes `taxi_out`.
3. **MEDIUM: FPS was below the floor of 12 at times.** It ranged from 10 to 20 fps with SwiftShader, a CPU renderer. That's only useful for comparing runs, because a real GPU will be much faster.
4. **LOW: cash falls about $1.3–1.8M per in-game day** with 6 carriers and 2 halls ($12.4M down to $4.4M over 6 days). Income shows "$0/hr" at times, and news reports a "city levy $133,073". Worth a balance check, because no NaN appeared and `cash` stayed finite in every run.
5. **LOW, UX: the build tray (Pavement) is open by default and covers the bottom third of the apron.** The southern stands, where the A380 parks, are hidden behind it. That conflicts with the "UI must never block the view" rule.
6. **LOW: some events go unanswered.** "Refused firefighting plane… Fine $2,500" and emergency asks pile up when nobody answers them. The bot doesn't handle these yet. It isn't a game bug, but the AT-802 never spawned because of it.

**Clean in every run:** no console or page errors on any branch (once the vite cache was warm), no NaN cash or stats, zero lost passengers in prefabs, **zero stuck flights** (per-phase timers) and **zero stuck service vehicles** (motionless for more than 240 game-seconds).

## Bot limitations
- The bot drives most of the setup through `window.__hatch` (dev only), not mouse clicks. Only the new-game, start and starter-kit steps use the real UI.
- A jet's airframe is re-rolled inside its kind after spawn, so pax and bag units come from the original pick.
- Ticking happens in-page, which leaves gaps in the rendering, so the videos are short, time-lapse style clips.
